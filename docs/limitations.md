# Limitations

- Single well-mixed thermal zone and first-order envelope dynamics.
- Idealized heating and cooling control, no humidity, ventilation, infiltration, thermal bridges, radiant asymmetry, or detailed plant loops.
- Constant COP and fan power, no part-load curve, cycling loss, defrost, economizer, storage, demand charge, or fault model.
- Occupancy and solar gains are simplified proxies.
- Calibration may be non-identifiable when inputs lack excitation.
- Jacobian uncertainty is local and excludes structural uncertainty.
- Comfort is a temperature-band proxy, not PMV, PPD, adaptive comfort, or a code-compliance assessment.
- Bundled data are synthetic and cannot support production-readiness claims.
